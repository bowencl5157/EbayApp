# Privacy Policy

## Effective Date: May 31, 2026

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how our eBay pricing and analytics application ("the Application") collects, uses, and protects your information.

### 2. Information We Collect
The Application may collect the following information:
- **OAuth Authorization Tokens**: When you connect your eBay account, we store access tokens to retrieve your seller data
- **Application Usage Data**: We may collect analytics on how you use the Application to improve our services
- **API Data**: We retrieve product pricing, keyword data, and seller analytics from eBay and Walmart APIs

### 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Provide pricing analysis and analytics features
- Display your eBay seller account data (sales, fees, profit)
- Compare pricing across eBay and Walmart platforms
- Generate SEO keyword analysis
- Improve the Application's functionality

### 4. Data Storage and Security
- OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device/server
- We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties
- We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information
- API data is processed in real-time and not permanently stored unless you choose to save it

### 5. Third-Party Services
The Application integrates with:
- **eBay API**: For product data, pricing, and seller account information
- **Walmart API**: For product pricing and affiliate commission data
- **Cloudflare Tunnel**: For secure OAuth redirect handling

These third parties have their own privacy policies which you should review.

### 6. Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Access your stored data
- Request deletion of your data
- Revoke eBay OAuth authorization at any time
- Opt out of data collection where applicable

### 7. Contact Information
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Application's support channels.

### 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the date at the top of this policy.
